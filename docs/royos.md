# Roy OS

The desk screen next to Roy all day (16-inch ASUS ZenScreen, 1920×1080, landscape). A Cloudflare Worker named `royos`.

## What is on the screen
- **Stage:** the board is a fixed 1920×1080 stage (`.board`, tiles placed in px) scaled to the window by `fitBoard`, so the ZenScreen shows it 1:1. The week's world fills the whole screen behind see-through tiles (light black glass, a soft blur, a text shadow), with darker edges and corners only. The tiles drift 1–2 px every 4 minutes against OLED burn-in. In portrait or below 900 px the tiles flow as one column over the world.
- **Menu bar:** Roy OS menu (this week's world, wallpaper, full screen, refresh, sign out), one icon per app, four links to the quest pages on the admin dashboard Worker (Quest log `/`, Journal `/journal?desk=1` (the main quest hidden), Fitness `/gym`, Admin `/admin` at admindashboard.quest-engine.workers.dev; each opens in its own reused tab, as the dashboard sends `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and has its own password login, its cookie SameSite=Lax since 6 Oct 2026 so a link from here arrives signed in), a "now" pill with Roy's current plan block and a countdown (tap: Steph and the kids), date and time.
- **Left column:** clock, date, greeting; Focus (today's win-if from the journal, open must/can to-dos; the win-if is the game's: it turns green with a check when the boss card's win for today's game day is Done (`/boss` sends it as `win` {day, done, hit}, not in its habits list; `win` {done, dmg} in `/data`, `winToday`); a tap opens the boss card popup to tick it off there, with its damage, and when the card closes the board refreshes and celebrates a win that landed); Showdown, titled "Goku vs <boss>": two stacked rows, the boss (level, HP bar, HP) and Goku (level, power level, HP bar with the ki shield in blue, XP bar), plus how many of the five main habits are done today (hover lists them). Tapping the boss row opens the boss card, the Goku row the Goku card, in a popup at the iPhone's size (402 × 874 points) with a Boss / Goku switch; the board refreshes when it closes. Habits are ticked on the boss card only, never from Roy OS. Me today: the recovery ring, the load ratio with its zone and bar, ki charge pips, this week's training hours against the target.
- **Centre:** the world, and the focus quest's quote with its author, handwritten beside the character (tap: Quest). Tap the world: a tap on the scene itself (not a tile, the menu bar, the clock or the quote) shows a card with the week's scene, show and character, a paragraph about the scene and one about the character (each one's `canonical_identity` from the quest catalogue, cut at a sentence near 420 characters) and the scene's `signature_features` as chips (`loreFor` in `src/themes.js`, `theme.lore`); a tap or Escape closes it, otherwise it goes after 25 s. Nothing is generated for it.
- **Focus timer:** the ▶ 25 min button on the Focus tile starts a 25-minute session, not tied to a to-do. The world dims around a ring over the character that fills up as the time runs (`.fdim`, `#fRing`), the other tiles fade back, and the now pill shows the time left. While it runs nothing else on the page takes a tap or a key, tap the world included; only the ring (tap: pause) and its Pause and Stop buttons do. The running session lives in the browser (`localStorage` `royos.focus`), so a refresh keeps it. Every finished or stopped session of at least a minute is saved with `POST /act {type: focus}` to the `focus` table in `DeskState` (day, start time, minutes, full or not) and kept for good; `/data` sends today's as `focus`.
- **Right column:** the weather (`WEATHER_PLACE`; the tile is a small animated sky painted in the week's world colours (the theme's accent, second colour and tint), brighter or duller with the weather and the hour (dawn, day, dusk, night), the sun or moon rides an arc from sunrise to sunset, clouds drift across, rain, snow, fog and lightning fill it, stars at night; on it the temperature, the conditions and then rain on its way or the feels-like, today's range as a bar with the temperature now as a dot, the wind, and the next 12 hours as a glowing temperature curve over a bar per hour for the rain chance, the wettest hour's chance shown when it reaches 30%; hover for the details, sunrise and sunset); Coming up (the next three dates; the window lists 30 days); From Steph (journal to-dos tagged "Steph"); Mood (five faces, a tap logs one; a tap on the tile opens today's timeline); Commute (morning and afternoon place, the ride, the year's share of work days in Belgium as a ring and days to spare; its window also lists the missing days: past weekdays this year with no morning or afternoon place in `work_location`, never today or a weekend row; a tap on a day opens its morning, afternoon and ride, and Save keeps them in that day's DeskState row (`POST /act {type: work, day}`), after which the share and the missing list count it. The quest D1 stays read only, so the journal's border page does not see these).
- **Bottom:** Your day, three lanes (Roy, Steph, Kids) from 06 to 23 with one now line, editable, with today's timed events from the family calendar, Steph's work calendar and Roy's personal calendar (read only); Ask Claude, with a suggestion that changes every 10 minutes.
- Both side columns fit the screen to the pixel: their tiles are spaced evenly and end 16 px above Your day (`fitLeft`, `stackCol`); Focus drops open to-dos one by one ("+N more") while the left column would not fit.
- Hover shows details; a click opens the app's window (Body, Focus, Mood, Commute, From Steph, Coming up, Quest), or for Showdown the card popup. Habit nudges (due now, still open) open the boss card.
- The light of day: the menu bar and the windows carry a faint tint that follows the real sun from the weather data (amber at sunrise, neutral by day, orange at sunset, cool blue at night). The world is never tinted. `body[data-light]` is dawn, day, dusk or night.
- The clip loops through a one-second crossfade (two copies take turns), so the seam never jumps.
- The critters: the week's critter (or a drawn black cat when it has none), a drawn Zaun rat and a gecko live on the board as silhouettes lit by the world's accent colour (`critters`, `crStep`, `CR_NEXT` in `web/os.client.js`). They walk the top edges of the tiles and jump between them; the cat sits, grooms, sleeps and stretches, the rat scurries, sniffs and rears up, the gecko darts, freezes and climbs down the tiles' sides. The week's critter (`theme.critter`) takes the cat's place: its three flare sheets are cut apart in the browser (`crCut`, `crFind`: per half of each sheet the four widest runs of painted columns), sized alike by their standing frames, and played one frame at a time (8-frame walk, sit with a glance, groom, breathing sleep, stretch, a leap frame by frame along the jump's arc); without it, or when a sheet can't be cut, the drawn cat walks. They never take a tap, and hide at night, in the phone layout and with reduced motion.
- OLED care, the night scene: from 23:00 to 06:30 the tiles hide and the world dims to a fifth of its light with the clip paused. A small dim clock with the date and the next calendar item moves to a new spot every 2 minutes (`moveNight`), and the dim world shifts a few pixels each time. A tap wakes the board for 5 minutes. The morning brief comes at 06:30.

## Routes
All need the desk cookie unless noted. JSON is `{ok, code, message, ...}`.
- `GET /` the page. `GET /login`, `POST /login` (password, next), `POST /logout`.
- `GET /data` everything the page shows (polled every minute). `GET /theme` this week's world and next week's pick.
- `POST /redo {what: clip|all, week}` (desk cookie) the menu's Redo buttons: `clip` makes a new clip from the week's still (`ensureTheme` retry: still and critter kept), `all` a new still and clip with the same character (force). Only this week or next week; refused while the week is being made. Paid, so the button needs a second tap within 5 seconds. Works from a preview too (it redoes the week shown).
- `GET /?at=2026-10-12T07:00` a preview: the board as it will be at that time (Amsterdam; a bare day is 07:00; within 60 days). The page's clock runs on from there, `/data?at=` feeds it, and an amber strip says it is a preview, with the world that week's Monday run will pick (`pickTheme`, nothing generated) and a link back to live. Read only: no edit, focus session or Ask is saved (`post` refuses in the browser), no calendar or weather cache is written, and no world, critter or commute sync is started. The calendars are read for that day; the weather is that day's Open-Meteo forecast at that hour; the game, recovery and the journal are what they are now (the journal and training for a day to come are empty, as on a real Monday morning). This week's world stays on until the real run makes the new one.
- `POST /act` one edit: `{type}` = `todo_tick {text, done}`, `todo_add {text, list}`, `mood {mood, note}`, `work {am, pm, commute}`, `plan {events}`, `work {day, am, pm, commute}` with a past `day` this year (fills in that day), `undo`, and `focus {day, at, min, done}` (a focus session: today or yesterday, `HH:MM`, 1 to 25 minutes; saved in DeskState's `focus` table, not part of the day's edits or undo; answers with today's sessions). There is no habit edit: habits are ticked on the boss card.
- `GET /card/boss`, `GET /card/goku` the boss card and the Goku card (rdecaste/Boss and rdecaste/MainQuest `index.html`), fetched over the service bindings `BOSS_CARD` (Worker `boss`) and `GOKU_CARD` (Worker `mainquest`), which skip their Cloudflare Access login; served same origin behind the desk cookie, framable by this page only (`X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`). The cards read and write the quest engine themselves; a boss card tap asks for the card passcode once on this screen (kept in this browser).
- `POST /ask {text}` the Ask box: the board as JSON plus Roy's words go to the chat model, which answers with a reply and actions; the actions are applied through the same code as `/act`, with one undo.
- `GET /status` (no cookie) `{ok, week, theme}` for healthchecks.
- Admin, header `X-Admin-Token`: `GET /theme/list`, `POST /theme/run` (`week`, `force=1`, `character=<name>` to remake the week with that character, its franchise and a seeded scene of it; as JSON `{"week":"2026-W41","force":true}`: the number 1 is not read as force), `POST /theme/retry` (`week`).

## Data
- Quest engine over the service binding (`/boss`, `/hero`, `/mainquest`, `/questboard`, and `/recovery` with `QUEST_ENGINE_TOKEN`): the load ratio with its zone and TSB (`power.load_ratio`), the morning's recovery and the last 7 mornings (`/recovery`, the readiness rules), and main habits and their 14-day history (good hits only), the load series (`power.load`, fitness and fatigue times a factor that is read off today's `power.fitness`), today's fitness, fatigue and form state, the week's peak, ki charge, the focus quest.
- Quest D1, read only: `journal` + `journal_focus` (win-if, `did_it_happen`, must/can), `todos` tagged Steph, `work_location` (today, the year's Belgium share and the missing days, `border.missing`), `workouts` joined with `workout_streams` (this week's sessions with hrTSS, average HR and minutes per zone; Z1 ≤145, Z2 146–162, Z3 163–173, Z4 174–184, Z5 185+). The per-second streams in R2 are not used.
- Weather: Open-Meteo for home (current, hourly for 12 hours, daily range, sunrise and sunset), shaped by `weatherView`, cached 15 minutes.
- Calendars: the published iCloud feeds in `FAMILY_ICS_URL` (the family calendar: the Kids lane, and Coming up) `STEPH_ICS_URL` (Steph's work shifts: her lane only, as work blocks, never in Coming up) `ROY_ICS_URL` (Roy's personal calendar: his lane and Coming up) and, when set, `KIDS_ICS_URL` (the kids' own calendar: their lane and Coming up), webcal read as https, parsed by `src/calendar.js` (timed and all-day events; DAILY/WEEKLY/MONTHLY/YEARLY rules with INTERVAL, UNTIL, COUNT, BYDAY, BYMONTH, BYMONTHDAY; EXDATE; RECURRENCE-ID overrides; Apple's Europe/Brussels and Europe/Paris stamps read in Amsterdam time). `agenda` gives today's timed events as lane blocks and 30 days of dates. Each feed has its own lane: family → Kids, Roy's → Roy, Steph's → Steph. (`laneOf` and the `LANE_WORDS` secret, which chose a lane from names in the title, are no longer used by the feeds.) Cached 10 minutes in the Durable Object; nothing is written to the calendar.
- The boss widget (`bossView`): from `/boss` the boss's `boss_name`, `epithet`, `boss_level`, `current_hp`, `max_hp`, `status` and the live hero HP (`hero_hp`, `hero_max_hp`); from `/mainquest` Goku's `level`, `level_xp`, `level_xp_max`, `power.power_level`, `power.shield.amount`, `power.ki`; the form from `/hero` `now.form`. Read only.
- The day's edits (ticks, added to-dos, moods, the plan) are kept per Amsterdam day in the `DeskState` Durable Object. The journal stays the record of the day; nothing is written to D1 from here, except commute days: a place or ride set on the screen (today, a missing day, or the Ask box) goes to the admin dashboard's `POST /border/save` over the `ADMIN_BOARD` binding, signed in with its cookie scheme (`ADMIN_PASSWORD` = its `DASHBOARD_PASSWORD`), and Roy OS drops its copy once the board has it (`src/work.js`). Days set before this existed are caught up on the next `/data`, filling only fields the board leaves empty; a day the board refuses stays in DeskState and is tried again. Focus sessions have their own `focus` table in the same Durable Object, one row per session, kept across days.
- Privacy: `safeText` drops any text containing a `DESK_HIDE` word (default `fap,pmo`), so the main quest never shows.

## The weekly world
- Week = ISO week of the Amsterdam date. Before Monday 06:00 the previous week's world is still current.
- Pick: seeded by the week (`rng('roy-os:' + week)`): random franchise among those with desk-worthy scenes and enabled characters (from the `characters` and `scenes` tables), then a random scene and a random character of that franchise. `NOT_FOR_DESK` lists the scenes skipped. The palette per franchise is in `PALETTES`; the page sets `--accent`, `--second`, `--tint` from it.
- Generation (`DeskTheme` Workflow): the still with the quest engine's image model `gpt-image-2.5-flare` (`IMAGE_MODEL`; edit mode with the character's avatar as reference, 2048×1152 (16:9 like the screen; 1536×1024 if the model refuses that size), high, moderation low), uploaded to Cloudinary `Desk-Themes/<week>-still-<stamp>` (a new name each time since 6 Oct 2026, so a redo never overwrites an earlier still); the clip with Veo 3.1 `veo-3.1-generate-preview` at 1080p since 6 Oct 2026 (`VIDEO_MODEL`; the quest engine's `gemini-omni-flash-preview` makes 1280×720 clips, soft on the screen, and comes back with that var: Interactions API, the still's 16:9 centre crop by URL, 16:9 out), uploaded as `Desk-Themes/<week>-clip-<stamp>`. `IMAGE_MODEL` starting with `gemini-` switches the still to Nano Banana, a `VIDEO_MODEL` starting with `veo` switches the clip to Veo (still as first and last frame, 1080p). Veo refuses prompts that name a character, so the clip prompt never does. Status goes `running → still → ready`, or `failed` with the error.
- The week's critter (after the clip, in the same run; `DeskTheme.critter`): the chat model picks a small four-legged fantasy creature that belongs in the week's world (`critterPickMessages`, cleaned by `critterFromAnswer`, a black cat if the answer is unusable). Flare paints it as three transparent 1536×1024 sheets of 8 frames in a 4 by 2 grid (`critterPrompts`): `walk` (one stride), then `rest` (sit, glance, paw up, lick, asleep, breathing in, standing, stretch) and `leap` (standing, crouch, push off, rise, mid-air, come down, land, settle) with the walk sheet as reference. Each is one paid image, uploaded to `Desk-Themes/<week>-critter-<sheet>`; the result is kept in the Durable Object as `critter:<week>`, and `/data` gives `theme.critter` (`name`, `walk`, `rest`, `leap` URLs) only when all three exist. A critter failure leaves the world ready. `CRITTER_ART` (`<week>|<ISO time>`) makes a week's critter once outside the Monday run; `<week>|<sheet>|<ISO time>` repaints only that sheet, keeping the creature and the other sheets.
- Cron `5 * * * *`: each hour `ensureTheme` starts the week's generation if the week has no row or its last try failed; running and ready weeks are left alone, unless `REMAKE` asks for a remake of this week (`remakeDue`). `POST /theme/run` with `force=1` remakes a week.
- Prompts: `stillPrompt` (scene and character rows word for word, the character busy in the scene (leaning on a railing, at a stall, on a device), seen from the side or three-quarters and never facing the viewer, no pin-up or glamour posing, the reference image for identity only, a settled pose (the clip holds the pose, so a mid-stride one looked frozen), a wide shot with the character about two thirds of the frame's height, cut around the knees or thighs (half the height made her too small, Roy 6 Oct 2026), subject in the middle third, the bottom fifth unimportant, both outer quarters dark for the side panels, open space right of the head for the quote) and `clipPrompt` (quest-engine's living-wallpaper rules: locked camera with no pan, zoom or push-in, the character anchored in place; a living character in place (breathing, blinking, glances, expressions, a head tilt, a small gesture) and a living scene (hair, cloth, signs, haze, rain, reflections), no steps or large moves; every motion returns to its start so the first and last frame match; the character is never named).

## Operations
- Secrets: `DESK_PASSWORD`, `ADMIN_TOKEN`, `ADMIN_PASSWORD` (the admin dashboard's DASHBOARD_PASSWORD, for commute days), `QUEST_ENGINE_TOKEN` (the engine's ADMIN_TOKEN), `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `CLOUDINARY_API_KEY`, `CLOUDINARY_API_SECRET`, `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `FAMILY_ICS_URL`, `STEPH_ICS_URL`, `ROY_ICS_URL`, `KIDS_ICS_URL` (optional), `LANE_WORDS`.
- Deploy: push to `main` (Workers Builds runs `npm test`, then deploys). The first deploy creates the Durable Object and the Workflow.
- First world: `curl -X POST https://royos.<account>.workers.dev/theme/run -H "X-Admin-Token: …"`, then watch `GET /theme/list`.
